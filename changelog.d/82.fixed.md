Read and write files as UTF-8 instead of the locale encoding. On Windows, where `locale.getpreferredencoding()` is cp1252, `summonpot add skills` decoded the packaged skill as cp1252 and wrote mojibake into `CLAUDE.md`, the cursor and windsurf rules, `.clinerules`, `copilot-instructions.md`, and `AGENTS.md` — and corrupted an existing `AGENTS.md` on its read-modify-write. Every `Path.read_text()` and `Path.write_text()` call now passes `encoding="utf-8"` explicitly.
